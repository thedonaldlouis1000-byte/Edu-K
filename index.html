Terms & Privacy 📜

1. Introduction & Mission

Welcome to Edu-k 2026. By using this platform, you agree to follow our learning rules designed to create a safe, fun, and educational environment for toddlers and students.

2. Privacy & COPPA Compliance

We respect the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do NOT collect location data, phone contacts, or sell personal info. Your name, stars, and unlocked rhymes are stored locally on your device or secured in our cloud only for your personal progress tracking.

3. Star & Reward System Rules

Stars are earned through genuine participation in ABCs, 123s, and Safari activities. Attempting to "hack" or manipulate the star count via browser consoles is a violation of our fair-play rules. Guest accounts are limited to a 10-star maximum until a verified account is created.

4. Content Usage (Rhymes & Videos)

The nursery rhymes and videos provided are for educational use only. Users are prohibited from downloading or redistributing our "Rhyme Cinema" content for commercial purposes. All custom Edu-K stories remain the intellectual property of the CEO (Louis).

5. User Conduct (The Kindness Rule)

Edu-K is a "Bully-Free Zone." While the current version is single-player, any future community features will require kindness. We reserve the right to suspend accounts that use inappropriate names or attempt to disrupt the learning hub.

6. Termination of Account

You may delete your account at any time via the Settings menu. Once deleted, all earned stars and unlocked "Locked Rhymes" will be permanently erased from our cloud sync.

7. Disclaimer

Edu-k is a learning aid, not a substitute for formal schooling. We strive for 100% uptime, but we are not responsible for stars lost due to cleared browser caches or device factory resets.

Our Mission 🍎

Edu-K (Education For Kids) was built on a single, powerful belief: that the bridge between play and academic excellence should be seamless. What started as a tool for toddlers to master the alphabet has evolved into a comprehensive learning ecosystem.

Whether it’s a Toddler tracing their first "A" through music and song, or an Explorer (Primary 1–5) solving complex BODMATH equations and publishing essays in their Portfolio—we ensure every milestone feels like a grand adventure.

By blending interactive AI guides with a global reward system, we help children build the confidence to lead and the curiosity to discover at their own unique pace. In the world of Edu-K, learning isn't just a task; it's a lifelong superpower.

Edu-k Help Center

What is Edu-k?

Edu-k is a fun learning tool for kids to master their ABCs and 123s through music and play!

Who created Edu-k?

Edu-k was founded by Louis Donald, a young developer passionate about educational tech.

Is it safe for children?

Yes! There are no ads or external distractions, making it a 100% safe environment for learning.

Does Edu-k collect personal data?

Privacy is our priority. We only store your name and photo locally on your device to personalize your experience.

What age group is this for?

Edu-k is perfect for toddlers and preschoolers (ages 2–6) starting their educational journey.

How does the Star Reward system work?

Kids earn stars for every letter or number they tap! It encourages them to keep learning through positive feedback.

Can I use Edu-k offline?

Once the website is loaded, most features like the ABCs and Numbers work perfectly without internet!

The sound is not working!

Check if your device is on silent. You can also toggle "Background Music" in the settings gear ⚙️.

How can I support Edu-k?

You can share the app with friends or use the "Support" link in the About section to help us stay free.

Are there more rhymes coming?

Yes! Our founder Louis is regularly adding new high-quality songs and rhymes to the collection.

Why did my stars stop at 10?

To save more than 10 stars, you need to Create a Profile! This ensures your progress is saved safely so you can unlock bigger rewards like Bedtime Stories.

How do I unlock Bedtime Stories?

Each magic story costs 30 stars. Once you earn enough by playing games, tap the story image to unlock it forever on your device!

What is "Tracing Mode"?

Tracing helps children develop fine motor skills. By following the ghost letters with their finger, they learn the actual motion of writing!

Why does the Pencil Guide appear?

If a child stops tracing for 4 seconds, our AI Instructor (the animated pencil) appears to show them exactly how to finish the shape.

Will I lose my stars if I logout?

Yes. For security and privacy, logging out wipes the current session data. To keep your stars, we recommend staying logged in on your personal device.

Is there a "Dark Mode" for night learning?

Yes! Tap the ⚙️ Settings Gear and toggle Dark Mode. This makes the screen softer on your child's eyes during bedtime stories.

Is my progress saved permanently?

Yes! Unlike other apps, once you Login, your stars, portfolio, and scores are synced to our cloud database. You can log in on any device and never lose your progress.

What is the Writing Portfolio?

When children complete an Essay or Letter in Word Quest, they can save it. Parents can view these later in the Hall of Fame and even Print them for the fridge!

How do I get into the Global Top 10?

The Hall of Fame ranks the top students globally based on their Star Count. Keep playing games and completing missions to climb the leaderboard!

Why did my stars stop at 10?

Guest accounts have a 10-star limit. To earn unlimited stars and unlock elite Bedtime Stories, you must Register for a free account.

What is the Brain Quiz?

It is a general knowledge challenge featuring 30 "Deep Knowledge" questions about Space, History, and Science to test how much your child really knows!

How do I turn on Dark Mode?

Tap the ⚙️ Settings Gear. Dark Mode is perfect for night learning or reading Bedtime Stories without straining your child's eyes.

How does the Pencil Guide work?

In Tracing mode, if the child stops for 4 seconds, our AI Instructor appears to show the correct movement of the letter or number.

Is there a cost or are there ads?

Edu-K is 100% Ad-Free. There are no sneaky "In-App Purchases." Every game and feature is built purely for safe learning.

Can my child learn in other languages?

Edu-k 2026 supports English, French, and Spanish! You can change the language in the Settings menu at any time.

Should I expect new upgrades and versions from Edu-K?

Edu-k 2026 version 2 is on the way with new improvement, because the producer is working on it. Remember to support using the account details and also share with friends to make Edu-K one of the best.
